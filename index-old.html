Why Forest Craft

A journey that began at one airport counter

Forest Craft began its journey in 2018 with a single store in Bengaluru International Airport, and has since grown into a retail and hospitality presence across India — built on skilled, village-based artisans and entire production processes, from drawing yarn to spinning and weaving, done by hand.

Every style carries dedication, creativity and quality — a deep-rooted commitment to celebrating the beauty of India's cultural heritage.
